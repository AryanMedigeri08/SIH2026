🎤 90-Second Verbatim Technical Pitch

1. Decoupled Tri-Tier Core Architecture (0s - 30s)

"Judges, enterprise credit requires zero tolerance for mathematical errors. That's why Udyam Saathi decouples computation into a strict Tri-Tier Core: 100% of banking math—including Nayak working capital and RBI-mandated DSCR—is computed in deterministic Python. Our spatial engine simultaneously maps 6.6 Lakh villages and 613 district amenities, feeding a 10-dimensional XGBoost classifier that delivers 98.9% cross-validation accuracy."

2. Lundberg TreeSHAP & Zero-Recalculation Guardrails (30s - 55s)

"Instead of an opaque black-box AI score, we use Lundberg TreeSHAP to compute exact game-theoretic factor attributions in under 2 seconds. When loan officers inspect a verdict, they see the exact rupee and demographic variables driving it. The LLM—Groq LLaMA 3.3 70B—is strictly restricted to multilingual narrative translation, protected by our Zero-Recalculation Invariant so no numbers can ever be hallucinated."

3. Sovereign Digital India BHASHINI & POOJA Engine (55s - 90s)

"To solve the rural literacy barrier, we integrated Digital India Bhashini across all 22 official Indian languages. Our voice co-pilot, POOJA, is not a simple chat wrapper—it has dynamic speech-to-action dispatch that allows users to verbally command UI navigation, switch dialects, and receive spoken solvency briefings. Combined with serverless Neon Postgres and containerized FastAPIs, our platform delivers an institutional-grade bank DPR for under 50 paise per execution."

🛡️ Technical Jury Q&A Cheat Sheet

Q1: "Why use XGBoost instead of a Deep Learning / Neural Network model?"
Defense: "Commercial credit underwriting demands explainability and auditability. Deep neural networks act as black boxes that cannot be defended to RBI auditors. XGBoost combined with TreeSHAP provides mathematically provable, game-theoretic feature attributions, while outperforming neural nets on tabular credit data."
Q2: "How does POOJA execute actions on the UI through voice?"
Defense: "POOJA uses an Action Registry schema in FastAPI. When a user speaks in any of the 22 Bhashini languages, intent classification identifies whether the user wants an informational query or a platform mutation. Actions like language switching or DPR section navigation are dispatched as structured JSON tool calls to the React frontend."
Q3: "How do you guarantee that the LLM doesn't alter financial figures in the DPR?"
Defense: "Our Zero-Recalculation Invariant: the LLM is passed pre-computed JSON financial tables and is prompt-locked to only draft qualitative explanatory text. The final PDF generator directly binds deterministic numbers into the statutory template, completely bypassing LLM manipulation."
